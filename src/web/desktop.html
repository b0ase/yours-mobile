Install it as an app

Installed, it gets its own window and dock or taskbar icon. Nothing to download from a store.

  1. Chrome or Edge: open desktop.bwalletx.com and click Install app at the top, or the install icon in the address bar.
  2. Safari on Mac: choose File › Add to Dock.
  3. Open bWalletX Desktop from your dock or app launcher like any other app.

Your keys stay in this browser

Your keys are kept encrypted in this browser on this computer. desktop.bwalletx.com is a separate site from web.bwalletx.com, so a wallet you made there or on another device does not appear here by itself: restore it with its recovery phrase. Keep your recovery phrase safe and offline.
