bWalletX features

One non-custodial BSV wallet for money, names, tokens, rooms, media and apps. Your keys never leave your device.

Android Chrome extension Web

Wallet

BSV, priced in dollars

Balances and prices shown in USD; you pay in sats. Live price chart.

Send to names

Pay a paymail like name@bwalletx.com instead of a long address.

Tokens and NFTs

Hold, send and receive BSV-21 tokens and 1Sat Ordinals, with a separate ordinals address.

Restore from anywhere

12 words are enough. Import from Yours, Panda, RelayX or Twetch, a private key or a backup file.

Sweep another wallet

Pull funds in from any 12/24-word wallet, scanning its addresses for you.

Find lost tokens

Recover purchases and tokens that didn't show up, and heal indexing.

bCredits

Top up credits for bCorp apps from the Wallet tab.

Several accounts

Keep separate accounts, each with its own name, token and room.

Names and identity

Your own paymail

Claim yourname@bwalletx.com and receive to it.

Verified X and Google names

Prove your X account or Gmail for a verified name.x or name.gmail.

Every name visible

Settings lists every name your wallet receives at, with one-tap unlink.

Profile and avatar

Pick an avatar and a display name that follow you into chat and the market.

Signed issuer statements

Every token you mint carries a statement signed by your identity, so anyone can check who made it.

Verified identity (optional)

KYC through bit-sign, where you choose what to reveal.

Make things

Your personal token

Mint a $HANDLE token named after you, in one tap.

Mint tokens

Create BSV-21 tokens with your own supply, icon and name.

Mint NFTs

Inscribe images, music and video as 1Sat Ordinals.

Tickets

Mint a ticket token; sending one invites someone into your room.

Social

Token rooms

Hold the token, join the room. Each token gets a chat room; the owner sets the rules.

Direct messages

Private chats with your contacts.

Voice and video calls

Call friends straight from the wallet.

Bounties

Post paid tasks in a token room and pay out from your wallet when they're done.

Feed

A feed of on-chain posts from BSV social apps, with bookmarks.

Lock to like

Back a post by time-locking your own BSV against it, plus a most-locked leaderboard.

Notifications

Payments, messages and room activity, with per-category controls.

Market and media

Market

Browse and buy tokens and NFTs, including every $HANDLE token and verified X accounts.

Sell

List your tokens and NFTs for sale from the wallet.

Safety filter

Always-on filter for unsafe content in the market; your own items blur instead.

Media player

Play music and video ordinals you own, with a mini player.

Shares

See company share listings in the market.

Apps and websites

bApps store

A built-in store of Bitcoin apps, one tap away.

Browser

Open BSV apps inside the wallet; they can ask it to pay or sign.

Connect to websites

A BRC-100 wallet sites can connect to. For developers.

Pair by QR

Scan a site's QR with your phone to connect it, and see or remove paired sites.

b agent

A help assistant: use your own AI key, or pay per message from the wallet.

Security

Non-custodial

Keys are created and stored on your device. We never see them.

Approve every request

Nothing is signed or paid without your tap; set a one-tap limit for small payments.

Face ID and fingerprint

Unlock with biometrics on your phone.

Strong passwords

Generate a strong password and change it anytime.

Delete your account

Remove your paymail, profile and messages from our servers whenever you like.
