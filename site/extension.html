bWalletX for Chrome

The full bWalletX wallet in Chrome's side panel, and a wallet websites like TokenBlaster can connect to. Your keys stay in the extension.

Download for Chrome (beta) ↓

It isn't on the Chrome Web Store yet, so install it by hand:

  1. Unzip bwalletx-extension-5.1.24.zip.
  2. Open chrome://extensions in Chrome.
  3. Turn on Developer mode (top right).
  4. Click Load unpacked and choose the unzipped folder.
  5. Pin bWalletX, click it, and create or restore your wallet.
