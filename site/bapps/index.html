The bApp standard

A bApp is a web app that lives inside bWallet, between the wallet's top bar and its tab bar. It brings no navigation of its own. It uses the wallet's identity, your $handle and payments instead of logins and card forms.

Charge in dollars. Users pay in sats.

Naming Convention

bApp names start with a lowercase b: bChat, bMail, bDrive. A bApp usually has its own BSV-21 token, $b<Name>, such as $bMail.

No b means a standalone venture, not a bApp.

Detection Live

bWallet's browser adds bWallet/1 YoursWalletMobile/1 to the user agent.

/\bYoursWalletMobile\/\d+/.test(navigator.userAgent)

Layout

  • No top bar, tab bar or back arrow of your own.
  • Respect safe areas; no position: fixed bars at the edges.
  • Dark theme: background #010101, gold #FFD24D.
  • Hosting inside the wallet frame (top bar h-14, tab bar 3.75rem) Proposed. Today apps open in a full-screen browser.

Identity Live

bWallet injects window.CWI, a BRC-100 wallet. Connect by asking for the identity key. No passwords, ever.

await CWI.waitForAuthentication({})
await CWI.getPublicKey({ identityKey: true })

Reading the user's $handle Proposed

Payments

Apps quote prices in USD. The wallet converts at the live rate and pays in sats. The user confirms, or the one-click limit they set applies.

Today: createAction in sats. A USD request, requestPayment({ usd }) Proposed

Back

The wallet owns Back. It walks your page history, then closes the app, so make your screens real history entries.

A back registry for your sheets, like the wallet's own useBackClose Proposed

Manifest: bapp.json Proposed

Name, short name, icon, start URL, category, permissions, USD price model, your token (ticker, tokenId), and your identity key, signed by that key.

Listing & the tick

bApps appear under Apps › bApps. Signed manifests and self-listing Proposed: a verified issuer's key earns the same tick their tokens get, and the listing links to the app's token market and room.

Security Live

The wallet takes each request's origin from the WebView, not from the page. Permissions are kept per origin. Apps never see keys or seeds.

Versioning

The spec is versioned 0.x while it's a draft. The user-agent number is the bridge version. Detect new methods by checking that they exist.

Full spec, manifest schema and a hello-world bApp: github.com/b0ase/bitcoinappsuite (coming soon).
