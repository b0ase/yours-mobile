Delete your bWallet data

bWallet (and bWalletX) is made by The Bitcoin Corporation Ltd. This page explains how to delete your account or some of your data, what is deleted, and what is kept.

Delete your account

  1. Open bWallet and unlock it.
  2. Go to Settings › Delete account.
  3. Confirm. Your account and the data our service keeps about it are deleted.

No longer have the app? Email support@bwalletx.com from any address with your $handle, and sign a short message we send you from your wallet if you still have your 12 words, so we know the account is yours. We reply within 30 days.

Delete some data, keep your account

  • Notifications: turn off Settings › Notifications. Your phone's registration is deleted from our push server.
  • Messages: delete your own chat messages in the chat.
  • Feed posts: use Hide on your own post.
  • Everything on this phone: sign out in Settings, or uninstall the app. Your wallet keys never leave your phone, so this removes them. Keep your 12 words if you want the wallet back.
  • Anything else: email support@bwalletx.com.

What is deleted, and what is kept

  • Deleted: your account, $handle and name, chat messages and photos you sent through our service, your notification settings and device registration, and your saved preferences.
  • Kept by the blockchain, not by us: transactions, tokens, NFTs, and posts or seals you wrote to the BSV blockchain are public and permanent. Nobody can delete them. We stop showing posts you hide.
  • Kept for safety: reports about abuse, and records we must keep by law, for as long as the law requires.
  • Identity checks: if you verified your identity, Veriff keeps the check under its own privacy policy. We delete our record of the result with your account.
  • Backups: deleted data can stay in our nightly database backups for up to 30 days, after which those backups are deleted.
