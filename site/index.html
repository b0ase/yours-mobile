Security

Non-custodial by design

  • Your keys are created and stored on your device, in the iPhone Keychain or Android Keystore, encrypted with your password.
  • Keys are never included in cloud backups and never sent to The Bitcoin Corporation Ltd.
  • Optional Face ID, Touch ID or fingerprint unlock. Your password is still needed for backups and sensitive settings.
  • Every app connection, payment and signature asks for your approval first.
  • Write down your recovery phrase. Nobody, including us, can recover it for you.
