Yours Wallet Mobile Experimental

Unofficial. This is an experimental mobile build of the open-source Yours Wallet, made by an independent developer. It is not made, reviewed or endorsed by the Yours Wallet team. It's in early testing: use a new wallet with small amounts only, and keep your recovery phrase safe.

Yours Wallet, on your phone.

BSV, 1Sat Ordinals and MNEE on iPhone and Android, built from the Yours Wallet code. It includes an in-app browser for BSV apps, and Face ID or fingerprint unlock. Your keys stay on your device.

What's different from the extension

Same wallet underneath

The wallet logic, key handling and permission prompts are the Yours Wallet extension's code, unmodified, packaged for mobile.

In-app browser

BSV apps open inside the wallet and connect over the same BRC-100 interface as the extension. You approve every request.

Keys stay on the device

Stored in the iPhone Keychain or Android Keystore, encrypted with your password, and never included in cloud backups.

Face ID and fingerprint

Optional biometric unlock. Your password still works, and is still needed for backups and sensitive settings.

Joining the beta

  1. iPhone: install Apple's TestFlight app, then open the TestFlight link above.
  2. Android: download the APK above and open it. Allow "Install unknown apps" for your browser when asked.
  3. Create a new wallet for testing, and report problems on GitHub.
