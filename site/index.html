How to sideload on Android
  1. Tap Download APK (beta). If Android asks, allow your browser to install unknown apps (Settings › Apps › your browser › Install unknown apps).
  2. Open the downloaded bwalletx-android-5.1.47.apk and tap Install.
  3. Optional but recommended: check the SHA-256 checksum matches
    cc86b24c734066aedae97e0a21b26c6937f87c01a059371e23e445d3063a088f

Security

Your keys. Your device. Your call.

Non-custodial

We never hold your coins. Your recovery phrase is yours alone and nobody can freeze or move your funds.

Face ID

Unlock and sign with Face ID or your device passcode. Keys stay encrypted on your phone.

Approve every request

Every app connection, signature and payment shows you what is happening before you say yes.

Identity

Verified identity, on your terms.

KYC via bit-sign

Verify once with bit-sign, powered by Veriff. You get a private certificate that lives in your wallet.

You choose what to reveal

Apps only see the details you approve. Nothing is shared by default.
