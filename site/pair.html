Connect with bWallet

This code connects a website on your computer to the bWallet app on your phone.

  1. Open bWallet on this phone.
  2. Tap the menu (top left), then Scan to connect a website.
  3. Scan the code on your computer again, check the 4-digit code matches, and tap Connect.

Using the bWalletX Chrome extension? It picks this link up and opens the pairing check in its side panel (or open bWalletX and paste the link under Connect the CLI / an AI assistant).

Codes expire after two minutes; the website shows a fresh one automatically.
