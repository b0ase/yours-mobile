bWalletX for Android

The full bWalletX wallet on your Android phone or tablet. Your keys stay on your device.

Download APK (beta) ↓

It isn't on Google Play yet, so install it by hand:

  1. Tap Download APK on your Android device.
  2. If Android asks, allow your browser to install unknown apps (Settings › Apps › your browser › Install unknown apps).
  3. Open bwalletx-android-5.1.24.apk and tap Install.
  4. Open bWalletX and create or restore your wallet.

Updating from an older version? Install over the top and your wallet stays. If Android says the app can't be updated, write down your 12 words first, then uninstall and reinstall.
