The Market

In bWallet, the App Store and Google Play edition, the Market is for browsing: see what tokens and NFTs exist, look at prices and play previews.

What you can do

Browse

Tokens, people's $HANDLE tokens, music, video, images and documents, filtered for safety.

Hold and send

Anything already in your wallet can be held, sent and played.

Want to buy and sell?

Trading is in bWalletX, the full edition for Android, Chrome and the web.
